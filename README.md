Friendly Message to Russia, China, and Pakistan:
Dear friends of Russia, China, and Pakistan,
We, the people of Iran, sincerely thank you for your support during these difficult times. We know that you are important allies and friends to Iran.
We kindly and respectfully ask you to continue standing with Iran. Please use your diplomatic power and influence to help us reach a fair and permanent agreement that protects Iran’s rights and brings lasting peace to the region.
We do not want war. We want peace with dignity and respect. We hope that with the help of our friends — Russia, China, and Pakistan — we can stop this conflict and achieve a stable, long-term solution that benefits everyone.
Your support means a lot to the Iranian people. We believe that together, through diplomacy and friendship, we can end this war and build a better future.
Thank you from the bottom of our hearts.
May peace prevail.
